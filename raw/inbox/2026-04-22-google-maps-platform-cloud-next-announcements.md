# New geospatial analytics and generative AI capabilities — Google Cloud Next

Google Maps Platform announced new generative AI capabilities and geospatial analytics datasets at Cloud Next.

## Headline announcements

- **Maps Imagery Grounding** (Private Preview) in Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform — empowers developers and enterprise teams to transform real world locations (starting with US locations) into cinematic realities using Gemini models + Geo's real-world location imagery, beginning with **Street View**.
- **Grounding with Google Maps** in Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform extends beyond location data with new **routing and Search Along Route** capabilities for reliable real-world decision-making.
- **Grounding Lite** is now **Generally Available** — a lightweight entry point for developers to ground AI agents with trusted Google Maps data through the **Google Maps MCP server**.

A broader suite of new geospatial analytics solutions, powered by **BigQuery and Google Earth AI**, helps transform planetary information into actionable intelligence.
