# Undeliverable: test2

Your message to **ramon@jairosoft.dev** couldn't be delivered. *ramon* wasn't found at *jairosoft.dev*.

- **Action Required:** Recipient unknown
- **Status code:** 550 5.1.1

## How to Fix

- The address may be misspelled or may not exist.
- Re-send the message and retype the recipient address (don't use Auto-Complete suggestions).
- Contact the recipient to verify the address.
- Check for forwarding rules that may be misrouting messages.

## Email Admin Notes

This error occurs because the sender sent a message to an email address outside of Office 365, but the address is incorrect or doesn't exist at the destination domain. The error is reported by the recipient domain's email server.

Possible causes:
- Recipient address doesn't exist or isn't correct
- Directory sync issues in hybrid environments
- Misconfigured forwarding rules
- Mail flow / MX record misconfiguration
