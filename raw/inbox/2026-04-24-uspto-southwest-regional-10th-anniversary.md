# Celebrating 10 years of innovation with the USPTO Southwest Regional Outreach Office

The USPTO Southwest Regional Outreach Office has been instrumental in assisting American inventors and small businesses, fostering innovation throughout the region.

## Event Details

- **When:** Monday, May 11, 11:30 a.m. – 1 p.m. CT
- **Where:** Arts District Mansion, **Dallas, Texas** (in person)
- **Hosted with:** Dallas Bar Association Intellectual Property Section

This event coincides with USPTO's celebration of America's 250th anniversary.

## Featured speakers

- **John A. Squires**, USPTO Director
- **Andrei Iancu**, former USPTO Director
- **Laura Peter**, former USPTO Deputy Director
- **Hope Shimabuku**, Director, USPTO Southwest Regional Outreach Office
- **Dr. Julio Palmaz**, Inventor of the balloon-expandable stent and owner of the PALMAZ® mark
