# Special USPTO Hour — Authenticity: The Name of the Game (NFL Draft)

Join the USPTO on **Friday, April 24, 10-11 a.m. ET** for a special edition of *USPTO Hour* live from the **2026 NFL Draft®** in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, to celebrate **World IP Day** ("IP and Sports: Ready, Set, Innovate").

## Featured speakers

- **John A. Squires**, USPTO Director
- **Jack Wallace**, three-time Paralympian gold medalist
- **Reggie Calhoun Jr.**, Director of NIL and Revenue Sharing for Colorado Buffaloes football
- **Chelsea Miko**, NIL Advocate and Founder of Miko Brands
- **John F. Duffy**, Director, Center on Intellectual Property Law, University of Virginia School of Law

Trademarks transcend sports — brand value, jerseys, logos, licenses for franchises; personal branding, NIL protection for athletes; and avoiding the AI-deepfake blitz for both.
