# SBA Relief Available to Hawaii Businesses

In response to a Presidential disaster declaration (April 7), the SBA announced low interest federal disaster loans for Hawaii businesses, private nonprofits, and residents affected by severe storms, flooding, landslides, and mudslides occurring **March 10–24, 2026**.

## Eligible Counties

- **Full eligibility** (Physical + Economic Injury loans): Hawaii, Maui, and City & County of Honolulu
- **EIDL only**: Kalawao and Kauai

## Application Deadlines

- **Physical Damage:** June 10, 2026
- **Economic Injury:** January 11, 2027 (typical EIDL deadline)

Covers business property, inventories, supplies, machinery, and more.
