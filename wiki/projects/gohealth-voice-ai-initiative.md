**Status:** Active — Vendor Response Sheet submitted; awaiting next steps from GoHealth (target reply ~2026-05-01).

## Overview

GoHealth UC is evaluating vendors to deliver a Voice AI solution that addresses transfer-latency pain in their existing Amazon Connect IVR. Jairosoft submitted a complete Vendor Response Sheet on 2026-04-24 — 49 functional requirements addressed, outcome-based pricing, and two architecture options (one bridging Amazon Connect, one replacing it with Jairosoft-hosted PSTN ingress).

## Status now

Vendor Response Sheet delivered. Eli confirmed receipt 2026-04-24 and committed to returning next steps by end of next week (~2026-05-01). No outstanding deliverables on Jairosoft's side; the project is in waiting-for-response mode.

## Goals / scope

- Win selection as Voice AI vendor for GoHealth UC.
- Architecture options offered:
  - **IVR Options 1.0** — SIP bridge into existing Amazon Connect.
  - **IVR Options 2.0** — Jairosoft-hosted PSTN ingress (eliminates the transfer-latency issue surfaced 2026-03-04).
- Pricing model: outcome-based primary tier ($0.08 / $0.10 / $0.12 per AI Processing Minute by quarterly containment) with flat $0.10/min alternative.
