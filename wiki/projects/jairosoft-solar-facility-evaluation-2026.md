## Overview

On 2026-04-25 at 17:51-17:52 PT, Mark Colina forwarded **5 vendor solar proposals** for Jairosoft to a 3-person decision panel — Ramon (CEO), Marikriss Aseniero (leadership), Mary Grace Garcia (admin/finance). The proposals span two topologies and four sizing tiers:

| Topology | Sizes proposed |
|---|---|
| **Hybrid** (solar + battery + grid backup) | 24KW, 36KW |
| **Grid Tie** (solar + grid feed-back, no battery) | 20KW, 30KW, 36KW |

All five are .docx files (~146 KB each) with the naming pattern `Solar - Jairosoft {SIZE} {TOPOLOGY}.docx`. Filename tells us these are explicitly scoped to Jairosoft (not the Aseniero residence). The later Outlook-captured duplicates identify the vendor as [[orgs/goldenboy-water-systems]].

## Status now

Proposals received. No decision yet. The panel has to choose:
1. **Topology:** Hybrid (resilient, expensive) vs. Grid Tie (cheaper, no outage protection).
2. **Sizing:** 20–36KW range — depends on the facility's actual load profile.
3. **Vendor:** Current proposals are from **Goldenboy Water Systems**; competitive bids may still be needed.

## Goals / scope

- Evaluate the 5 vendor proposals.
- Determine the right system size based on facility load.
- Decide between Hybrid vs. Grid Tie.
- (Likely) solicit competing bids before committing to capex.
