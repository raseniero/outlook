# OpenGov Procurement

**Type:** Government procurement notification platform (parallel to Hawaii's HiePro but for non-HI state/local agencies nationwide).
**Sender:** `procurement-notifications@opengov.com`

## Overview

OpenGov surfaces in the corpus as a procurement-notification source where Ramon has **subscribed to specific issuers** (City of Taylor MI, Housing Authority of Birmingham AL). Notifications are issuer-based, not commodity-code-based — meaning Ramon explicitly opted in to those issuers at some prior point.

The two RFPs received 2026-04-23 (Birmingham Legal Services, Taylor MI Vacuum Breakers) are **clear non-fits** for Jairosoft (legal services, plumbing hardware). This suggests the OpenGov subscriptions are either stale or were originally for a different / earlier business interest.
